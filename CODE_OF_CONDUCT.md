# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a respectful experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Accept good-faith feedback and differing technical opinions.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Protect privacy; do not post credentials, private contact details, customer data, or private repository content.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, doxxing, publishing private information without permission, and deliberately disruptive conduct are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers may edit or remove content and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community. Security and privacy incidents should be reported privately rather than amplified in public discussion.
